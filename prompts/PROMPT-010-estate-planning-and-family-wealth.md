### Master Treatises in this Batch:

1. **`041-wills-succession-indian-succession-act-hsa-probate.md`**  
   - **Topic:** LegacyIndia — Wills, Intestate Succession & Probate Jurisprudence.  
   - **Scope:** Testamentary transmission of wealth under Indian Succession Act 1925: drafting a legally binding Will, two independent attesting witnesses requirement, execution capacity, revocation and codicils, testamentary versus intestate succession rules under the Hindu Succession Act 1956 (Class I versus Class II heirs) and Indian Succession Act, mandatory probate requirements in the presidency towns of Mumbai, Kolkata, and Chennai, and high-conflict partition disputes.

2. **`042-family-trusts-private-trusts-indian-trusts-act-1882.md`**  
   - **Topic:** TrustWealth India — Private Family Trusts & Ring-Fencing Assets.  
   - **Scope:** Private family trust structuring under the Indian Trusts Act 1882: Settlor, Trustee, and Beneficiary roles, Revocable versus Irrevocable trusts, Determinate versus Discretionary trusts, asset protection against potential business insolvency and matrimonial litigation, taxation of private trusts (taxation at Maximum Marginal Rate under Section 164 for discretionary trusts), stamp duty on trust deeds, and ongoing trustee fiduciary obligations.

3. **`043-nomination-versus-legal-heir-supreme-court-rulings.md`**  
   - **Topic:** NomineeLaw India — Nominee versus Legal Heir Jurisprudence.  
   - **Scope:** Landmark judicial rulings of the Supreme Court of India (including Ram Chander Talwar and Shakti Yezdani precedents) establishing that a nominee is merely a statutory trustee and temporary custodian appointed to receive funds from banks, insurance companies, or EPFO, holding them in trust for the ultimate beneficial legal heirs determined under testamentary succession or personal succession laws.

4. **`044-cross-border-assets-foreign-stocks-rbi-lrs-schedule-fa.md`**  
   - **Topic:** GlobalWealth India — Foreign Assets, Schedule FA & Black Money Act.  
   - **Scope:** Cross-border financial assets held by Indian residents: Reserve Bank of India Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS) annual ceiling of two hundred and fifty thousand US dollars, mandatory disclosure in Schedule FA (Foreign Assets) of the Income Tax Return regardless of threshold, draconian penalties under the Black Money (Undisclosed Foreign Income and Assets) and Imposition of Tax Act 2015 (flat thirty percent tax plus ninety percent penalty and rigorous imprisonment for non-disclosure), and foreign dividend withholding tax relief.

5. **`045-life-stage-wealth-architecture-student-midcareer-retirement.md`**  
   - **Topic:** LifeStages India — Dynamic Asset Allocation across Life Milestones.  
   - **Scope:** Systematic transitions in portfolio construction across life milestones in India: student to young professional (establishing six-month emergency liquidity reserves, securing pure term and health insurance, eliminating education debt), mid-career wealth accumulation (equity compounding, retirement savings acceleration, children higher education fund ring-fencing), and pre-retirement transition (glide paths, de-risking equity exposure to sovereign debt, and health buffer provisioning).
