### Master Treatises in this Batch:

1. **`046-rbi-monetary-policy-mpc-repo-corridor-liquidity.md`**  
   - **Topic:** CentralBank India — RBI Monetary Policy, Repo Corridor & Transmission.  
   - **Scope:** Monetary policy implementation by the Reserve Bank of India: the six-member Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) statutory framework under the RBI Act 1934, the flexible inflation target of four percent with a two percentage point tolerance band on either side, the policy repo rate, Standing Deposit Facility (SDF) and Marginal Standing Facility (MSF) corridor, systemic liquidity absorption and injection via variable rate repo auctions, and transmission friction to commercial bank lending and deposit rates.

2. **`047-aspirational-inflation-urban-lifestyle-education-healthcare.md`**  
   - **Topic:** LifestyleInflation India — Aspirational Urban Inflation Dynamics.  
   - **Scope:** The critical divergence between sovereign Consumer Price Index (CPI) metrics and true urban lifestyle inflation in India: headline CPI food and fuel skew versus eight to twelve percent annualized inflation in private school and tertiary education tuition, corporate healthcare procedure costs, Tier 1 metro residential rentals, and lifestyle aspiration creep, and the severe portfolio erosion caused by using headline CPI for retirement corpus planning.

3. **`048-interest-rate-cycles-gsec-yield-curve-duration-strategy.md`**  
   - **Topic:** RateCycles India — Sovereign Yield Curves & Duration Management.  
   - **Scope:** Macroeconomic interest rate cycles and bond market portfolio management in India: ten-year benchmark Central Government Security (G-Sec) yield dynamics, normal versus flat versus inverted yield curves, RBI open market operations (OMO) and Operation Twist, Macaulay duration and convexity management, and tactical portfolio shifts between money market funds and long-dated sovereign gilts across rate hiking and easing regimes.

4. **`049-rupee-depreciation-currency-risk-usd-inr-reer.md`**  
   - **Topic:** RupeeForex India — Currency Dynamics, USD-INR Depreciation & REER.  
   - **Scope:** Foreign exchange economics and purchasing power risks for Indian households: historical annualized three to four percent nominal depreciation of the Indian Rupee against the US Dollar, Nominal Effective Exchange Rate (NEER) versus Real Effective Exchange Rate (REER), crude oil import dependencies, current account deficit (CAD) pressures, RBI foreign exchange reserve interventions, and hedging cross-border liabilities such as foreign university tuition fees.

5. **`050-sovereign-debt-fiscal-deficit-frbm-banking-crr-slr.md`**  
   - **Topic:** FiscalIndia — Sovereign Borrowing, FRBM Act & Statutory Bank Ratios.  
   - **Scope:** Fiscal policy architecture of the Union of India: central and state fiscal deficits, gross market borrowing programs, targets under the Fiscal Responsibility and Budget Management (FRBM) Act 2003, statutory liquidity pre-emptions imposed on the banking system including Cash Reserve Ratio (CRR) and Statutory Liquidity Ratio (SLR), and the sovereign crowd-out effect on private corporate debt issuance.
