### Master Treatises in this Batch:

1. **`026-pure-term-insurance-human-life-value-section-45.md`**  
   - **Topic:** PureTerm India — Term Insurance, Human Life Value & Section 45 Incontestability.  
   - **Scope:** Pure risk life insurance: Human Life Value (HLV) calculation models (income replacement and expense-discounted methods), rejection of endowment and ULIP mis-selling, Section 45 of the Insurance Act 1938 (three-year incontestability rule protecting beneficiaries against claim repudiation), medical underwriting disclosures, riders (critical illness, accidental disability, waiver of premium), and claim settlement ratio versus claim amount settlement ratio.

2. **`027-health-insurance-super-top-up-restoration-claim-settlement.md`**  
   - **Topic:** HealthInsure India — Family Floater, Super Top-Up & Claim Settlement.  
   - **Scope:** Health insurance architectures under IRDAI regulations: individual versus family floater policies, base cover plus deductible-based super top-up combination economics, waiting periods for pre-existing diseases (PEDs), room rent sub-limits and proportionate deduction traps, cashless hospital networks, copayment clauses, and modern Daycare Procedure coverage.

3. **`028-critical-illness-cancer-cardiac-disability-riders.md`**  
   - **Topic:** CriticalCare India — Defined Illness Coverage, Staged Payouts & Disability.  
   - **Scope:** Catastrophic illness risk protection: standalone critical illness plans versus attached riders, defined survival period requirements (typically thirty days post-diagnosis), specified list of covered critical conditions (cancer of specified severity, first heart attack, stroke resulting in permanent symptoms, major organ transplant), lump-sum benefit release mechanics, and total permanent disability (TPD) income replacement.

4. **`029-motor-home-asset-insurance-zero-depreciation-idv.md`**  
   - **Topic:** AssetShield India — Motor Insurance, Insured Declared Value & Home Protection.  
   - **Scope:** General insurance for physical assets: motor insurance mandates under Motor Vehicles Act 1988, Third-Party Liability versus Own Damage, Insured Declared Value (IDV) depreciation schedules, Zero Depreciation (Bumper to Bumper) rider economics, Engine Protect and Return to Invoice covers, and standard home insurance policies (Bharat Griha Raksha) covering structure, contents, fire, earthquakes, and flood inundation.

5. **`030-microinsurance-pmjjby-pmsby-ayushman-bharat.md`**  
   - **Topic:** BharatSuraksha — Sovereign Microinsurance, PMJJBY, PMSBY & PMJAY.  
   - **Scope:** Government-sponsored social security insurance schemes in India: Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY) life cover (two lakh rupees for 436 rupees annual premium), Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY) accidental death and disability cover (two lakh rupees for 20 rupees annual premium), auto-debit rules, bank branch claim filing, and Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY five-lakh-rupee secondary and tertiary hospitalization coverage for vulnerable families.
