### Master Treatises in this Batch:

1. **`051-ponzi-schemes-chit-funds-banning-unregulated-deposits-act.md`**  
   - **Topic:** FraudShield India — Ponzi Schemes, Chit Funds & BUDS Act 2019.  
   - **Scope:** Forensic anatomy of fraudulent retail deposit schemes in India: the Banning of Unregulated Deposit Schemes Act 2019 (BUDS Act), criminal sanctions and absolute confiscation of assets, registered chit funds under the Chit Funds Act 1982 versus illicit multi-level marketing (MLM) and pyramid schemes under the Prize Chits and Money Circulation Schemes (Banning) Act 1978, red-flag indicators of guaranteed astronomical returns, and sovereign state-level enforcement authorities.

2. **`052-dabba-trading-illegal-derivatives-bucket-shops-scra.md`**  
   - **Topic:** DabbaBan India — Illegal Off-Exchange Bucketing & SCRA Penalties.  
   - **Scope:** Forensic investigation of dabba trading (unauthorized off-exchange bucketing): violations of Section 13 and Section 16 of the Securities Contracts (Regulation) Act 1956 (SCRA), Section 23 criminal penalties (up to ten years imprisonment and fines up to twenty-five crore rupees), complete absence of investor protection funds or clearing corporation guarantees, tax evasion forensics, and coordinated law enforcement raids with cyber police.

3. **`053-digital-lending-scams-predatory-apps-rbi-guidelines.md`**  
   - **Topic:** LendingDefense India — Predatory Loan Apps & RBI Digital Guidelines.  
   - **Scope:** Protection against predatory digital lending in India: RBI Master Directions on Digital Lending, regulatory mandates requiring all loan disbursals and repayments to flow directly between the borrower bank account and the regulated entity (Bank or NBFC) without third-party pass-through, prohibition against accessing mobile contacts, photos, and location permissions, and statutory borrower grievance escalation against harassment.

4. **`054-rbi-integrated-ombudsman-banking-nbfc-payments-redressal.md`**  
   - **Topic:** BankingOmbudsman India — RBI CMS & Three-Tier Redressal.  
   - **Scope:** Statutory dispute resolution for retail banking, NBFC, and digital payment customers under the Reserve Bank - Integrated Ombudsman Scheme 2021: mandatory thirty-day internal complaint window with Principal Nodal Officers, online complaint submission through the Complaint Management System (CMS), grounds for complaint, power of the Ombudsman to award compensation up to twenty lakh rupees for actual loss and one lakh rupees for mental anguish, and appellate mechanisms before the Executive Director of the RBI.

5. **`055-sebi-scores-smart-odr-insurance-ombudsman-council.md`**  
   - **Topic:** RedressalPortals India — SEBI SCORES 2.0, SMART ODR & Insurance Ombudsman.  
   - **Scope:** Capital markets and insurance dispute escalation channels: SEBI SCORES 2.0 platform integrated with automated time-bound routing to listed entities and intermediaries, the SMART Online Dispute Resolution (ODR) portal for conciliation and independent online arbitration, Council for Insurance Ombudsmen operating across seventeen regional centers under the Insurance Ombudsman Rules 2017, and thirty-lakh-rupee binding award jurisdiction against insurer claim repudiations.
