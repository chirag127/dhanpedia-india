### Master Treatises in this Batch:

1. **`056-financial-independence-retire-early-fire-india-context.md`**  
   - **Topic:** BharatFIRE — Financial Independence & Early Retirement in India.  
   - **Scope:** Adapting the Financial Independence, Retire Early (FIRE) movement to the Indian macroeconomic environment: Lean FIRE versus Fat FIRE versus Coast FIRE, accounting for Indian healthcare inflation (ten to fourteen percent per annum), extended parental dependency, lack of universal social security pensions, dynamic savings rates (fifty to seventy percent of net salary), and psychological post-retirement identity transitions.

2. **`057-safe-withdrawal-rates-sequence-of-returns-bucket-strategy.md`**  
   - **Topic:** WithdrawalMath India — Safe Withdrawal Rates & Three-Bucket Portfolios.  
   - **Scope:** Mathematical sustainability of retirement withdrawals in India: why the Western four percent rule fails in high-inflation emerging markets, sequence-of-returns risk during the first decade of retirement, dynamic withdrawal rules (Guyton-Klinger guardrails), and the Three-Bucket portfolio architecture (Bucket One for three years cash, Bucket Two for five to seven years fixed income, Bucket Three for long-term equity growth).

3. **`058-multi-generational-wealth-family-constitution-governance.md`**  
   - **Topic:** DynasticWealth India — Family Governance, Constitutions & Longevity.  
   - **Scope:** Preservation of family wealth across generations: the three-generation wealth dissipation curse, family offices in India, drafting a Family Constitution, Family Council meetings, financial education for next-generation inheritors, conflict resolution protocols, and balancing entrepreneurial capital deployment with conservative wealth preservation.

4. **`059-financial-freedom-systems-automation-cash-buffer-design.md`**  
   - **Topic:** WealthEngine India — Zero-Friction Cash Flow Automation Systems.  
   - **Scope:** Engineering a hands-off, automated personal finance operating system: salary day automatic fund allocation, systematic investments, recurring emergency fund replenishment, auto-debit utility and credit card bills with dual safety buffers, and eliminating cognitive friction and behavioral panic through pre-committed financial rules.

5. **`060-dhanpedia-master-compendium-unifying-60-domains.md`**
