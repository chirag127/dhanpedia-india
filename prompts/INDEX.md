## The 60-Domain Canonical Compendium Structure

Rather than fragmenting into 60 separate repositories, Dhanpedia India serves as the single sovereign master encyclopedia and book publishing engine. The entire Indian financial ecosystem across 60 specialized domains is authored into canonical master treatises and compiled into the Master Reference Book and Moon+ Reader Pro TTS Audiobook.

### Non-Negotiable Content & Audio-Native Rules Across All Prompts:
1. **Audio-Native Prose Mandate:** Canonical article bodies must consist strictly of natural-language prose organized into semantic headings (`#`, `##`, `###`) and connected paragraphs.
   - **Zero ASCII Art & Box Diagrams:** Never author ASCII box trees, arrows, or flowcharts (e.g., `└──`, `──>`, `│`, `▼`, `┌`, `└`, `─`).
   - **Zero Code Fences:** Never insert code blocks (` ``` `) in article bodies.
   - **Zero Raw Formulas:** Never use LaTeX blocks (`$$...$$`). Articulate all mathematical relationships, compounding mechanics, and financial formulas in flowing spoken English.
   - **Zero Data Tables or Table Traps:** Tabular data must be explained in comparative narrative paragraphs.
2. **Moon+ Reader Pro TTS Asterisk Stripping:** Markdown bold (`**word**`) is converted to styled HTML in visual pages and PDF, but in plain text and TTS audiobook generation all asterisks (`**` and `*`) must be stripped cleanly so Moon+ Reader Pro synthesizes pure natural spoken English without saying "asterisk" or "star".
3. **Corpus Scale & Depth:** Modular and rigorous (2,500 to 5,000+ words per master treatise; 5,000 to 10,000+ words for foundational pillar treatises). Zero padding, zero repetition, zero fluff.
