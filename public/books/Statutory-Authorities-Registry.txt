=== Verified Statutory Claims ===

- CLM-DICGC-LIMIT (legal_rule): Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation (DICGC) provides statutory insurance coverage up to a maximum limit of ₹5,00,000 per depositor for both principal and interest across all accounts held in the same right and capacity in an insured bank. Authority: DICGC Act 1961, Section 16(1), as amended by Gazette Notification dated February 4, 2020. Citations: SRC-DICGC-MAIN, SRC-RBI-MAIN.

- CLM-DICGC-SEC18A (legal_rule): Under Section 18A of the DICGC Act (inserted by 2021 Amendment Act), depositors of an insured bank placed under directions or moratorium by the RBI with withdrawal restrictions are entitled to receive interim insurance payments up to ₹5,00,000 within 90 days. Authority: Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation (Amendment) Act, 2021 (Act No. 30 of 2021), Section 18A. Citations: SRC-DICGC-MAIN, SRC-RBI-MAIN.

- CLM-ITD-269SS (tax_rule): Under Section 269SS of the Income-tax Act 1961, no person shall take or accept from any other person any loan, deposit, or specified advance relating to the transfer of immovable property in cash if the aggregate amount exceeds ₹20,000; violation triggers a 100 percent monetary penalty under Section 271D. Authority: Income-tax Act 1961, Section 269SS read with Section 271D and Section 273B. Citations: SRC-CBDT-MAIN, SRC-ITD-PORTAL.

- CLM-ITD-269ST (tax_rule): Under Section 269ST of the Income-tax Act 1961, no person shall receive an amount of ₹2,00,000 or more in cash in aggregate from a person in a day, in respect of a single transaction, or in respect of transactions relating to one event or occasion; violation triggers a 100 percent monetary penalty on the receiver under Section 271DA. Authority: Income-tax Act 1961, Section 269ST inserted by Finance Act 2017, read with Section 271DA. Citations: SRC-CBDT-MAIN, SRC-ITD-PORTAL.

- CLM-ITD-SEC50C (tax_rule): Under Section 50C and Section 56(2)(x) of the Income-tax Act 1961, if the stamp duty value (circle rate) of land or building exceeds the declared agreement consideration by more than 10 percent (safe-harbor tolerance band), the stamp duty value is deemed to be the full value of consideration for computing capital gains for the seller and taxable as other income for the buyer. Authority: Income-tax Act 1961, Section 50C(1) third proviso and Section 56(2)(x)(b)(B). Citations: SRC-CBDT-MAIN, SRC-ITD-PORTAL.

- CLM-SCRA-DABBA (legal_rule): Dabba trading (operating or trading in contracts in securities outside a recognized stock exchange) is prohibited under Sections 13 and 16 of the Securities Contracts (Regulation) Act 1956, and constitutes a criminal offense punishable under Section 23 with imprisonment for up to 10 years, a fine up to ₹25 Crore, or both. Authority: Securities Contracts (Regulation) Act 1956, Sections 13, 16, and Section 23(1). Citations: SRC-SEBI-MAIN, SRC-INDIA-CODE.

- CLM-BENAMI-ACT (legal_rule): Under the Prohibition of Benami Property Transactions Act 1988 (as amended in 2016), entering into a benami transaction is a cognizable criminal offense punishable under Section 53 with rigorous imprisonment of 1 to 7 years and a fine up to 25 percent of the fair market value of the property, with mandatory statutory confiscation of the property without compensation under Section 5. Authority: Prohibition of Benami Property Transactions Act 1988 as amended by Benami Transactions (Prohibition) Amendment Act 2016, Sections 3, 5, and 53. Citations: SRC-CBDT-MAIN, SRC-INDIA-CODE, SRC-ED-GOV.

- CLM-BUDS-ACT (legal_rule): The Banning of Unregulated Deposit Schemes Act 2019 (BUDS Act) completely bans all unregulated deposit schemes operating without registration under designated statutory regulators, making deposit-taking or advertisement a cognizable criminal offense punishable with imprisonment up to 10 years and fines up to ₹50 Crore under Section 21. Authority: Banning of Unregulated Deposit Schemes Act 2019 (Act No. 21 of 2019), Sections 3, 7, and 21. Citations: SRC-INDIA-CODE, SRC-RBI-MAIN, SRC-SEBI-MAIN.

- CLM-TAX-FA2024-CAPGAINS (tax_rule): Under the Finance (No. 2) Act 2024, short-term capital gains on listed equity shares, equity mutual fund units, and business trust units under Section 111A are taxed at 20 percent (up from 15 percent), while long-term capital gains under Section 112A are taxed at 12.5 percent (up from 10 percent) with an increased annual exemption threshold of ₹1.25 Lakh (up from ₹1 Lakh). Authority: Finance (No. 2) Act 2024 (Act No. 15 of 2024), amending Sections 111A, 112, 112A of the Income-tax Act 1961. Citations: SRC-CBDT-MAIN, SRC-ITD-PORTAL.

- CLM-EPF-CONTRIB-CEIL (regulatory_requirement): Under the Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act 1952, employees in covered establishments contribute 12 percent of their basic wage plus dearness allowance, with employers matching 12 percent (split into 8.33% to EPS subject to statutory wage ceiling and 3.67% to EPF), with mandatory coverage applicable to employees earning up to ₹15,000 monthly at the time of joining. Authority: EPF Scheme 1952, Paragraph 29 read with EPS 1995 Paragraph 3. Citations: SRC-EPFO-MAIN.

- CLM-RBI-P2P-AUG2024 (regulatory_requirement): Under the RBI August 16, 2024 Master Direction amendments for NBFC-P2P lending platforms, platforms are strictly prohibited from providing credit enhancements, assured returns, or structuring investment products with instant liquidity, tenure mismatch, or auto-reinvestment without explicit prior lender consent. Authority: RBI Master Direction - NBFC - Peer to Peer Lending Platform (Reserve Bank) Directions, 2017 (Updated as on August 16, 2024). Citations: SRC-RBI-MAIN.

- CLM-INS-SEC45 (legal_rule): Under Section 45 of the Insurance Act 1938 (as substituted by Insurance Laws (Amendment) Act 2015), no policy of life insurance shall be called in question on any ground whatsoever, including fraud or misrepresentation, after the expiry of three years from the date of issuance of the policy or the date of commencement of risk or date of revival, whichever is later. Authority: Insurance Act 1938, Section 45 as amended by Act No. 5 of 2015. Citations: SRC-IRDAI-MAIN, SRC-INDIA-CODE.
