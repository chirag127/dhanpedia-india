## 2. Category Specifications

### 01. Personal-Finance Foundations (`pf-foundations`)
- **Scope**: Time value of money, inflation dynamics (official CPI vs aspirational urban inflation), personal balance sheet engineering, cashflow budgeting, cognitive biases in Indian financial decision-making.
- **Key Questions**: How does inflation erode nominal wealth in India? How should a personal net worth statement be structured?
- **Regulators**: Reserve Bank of India.
- **Planned Articles**: `time-value-of-money-and-real-returns-india`, `personal-balance-sheet-and-cashflow-architecture`.

### 02. Indian Banking and Payments (`banking-payments`)
- **Scope**: Scheduled commercial banks, small finance banks, payment banks, UPI/NPCI settlement rails, NEFT/RTGS, sweep account mechanics, DICGC deposit insurance.
- **Key Questions**: How does DICGC guarantee ₹5 Lakh per depositor per bank across account capacities? How does daily product interest calculation function?
- **Regulators**: Reserve Bank of India (RBI), DICGC.
- **Planned Articles**: `dicgc-deposit-insurance-architecture-limits-and-ownership-structures`, `indian-retail-banking-mechanics-savings-interest-and-sweep-accounts`.

### 03. Credit Cards and Consumer Credit (`credit-cards-consumer-credit`)
- **Scope**: Credit bureaus (CIBIL, Experian, Equifax, CRIF High Mark), credit card statement cycles, grace periods, APR compound interest calculation, chargeback procedures under RBI Master Directions.
- **Key Questions**: What factors drive TransUnion CIBIL scores? How do finance charges accrue on revolving card balances?
- **Regulators**: RBI, Credit Information Companies Regulation Act (CICRA).
- **Planned Articles**: `cibil-score-mechanics-optimization-and-dispute-resolution`, `credit-card-billing-cycles-grace-periods-and-apr-mathematics`.

### 04. Emergency Funds and Financial Protection (`emergency-protection`)
- **Scope**: Non-discretionary burn rate estimation, multi-tier liquidity frameworks (cash, sweep deposits, arbitrage funds, liquid funds), operational redundancies during systemic outages.
- **Key Questions**: How many months of non-discretionary burn rate are required for Indian households across different employment models?
- **Regulators**: RBI, SEBI.
- **Planned Articles**: `emergency-fund-architecture-sizing-tiering-and-taxation`, `liquid-funds-vs-arbitrage-funds-vs-sweep-fixed-deposits`.

### 05. Income, Salary, and Career Capital (`income-career-capital`)
- **Scope**: Indian Cost-to-Company (CTC) architectures, basic vs allowances, EPF employer/employee contributions, gratuity calculation under Payment of Gratuity Act 1972, ESOP and RSU vesting and perquisite taxation.
- **Key Questions**: What is the net in-hand impact of standard CTC allowances? How are foreign stock vesting events taxed in India?
- **Regulators**: CBDT / Income Tax Department, EPFO.
- **Planned Articles**: `ctc-salary-breakdown-mechanics-tax-optimization`, `rsu-and-esop-taxation-for-indian-tech-professionals`.

### 06. Indian Taxes and Financial Compliance (`taxes-compliance`)
- **Scope**: Direct taxation under Income-tax Act 1961, Section 115BAC (New Tax Regime) vs Old Tax Regime, Capital Gains schedules (STCG Section 111A, LTCG Section 112A), TDS under Sections 194A/194DA/194IA, advance tax deadlines and Section 234A/B/C interest penalties.
- **Key Questions**: How to choose between Old and New Tax regimes mathematically? What are the post-Finance Act 2024 capital gains rules?
- **Regulators**: Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT), Ministry of Finance.
- **Planned Articles**: `indian-income-tax-slabs-old-vs-new-regime-decision-framework`, `capital-gains-tax-india-complete-guide-finance-act-2024`.

### 07. Mutual Funds and Exchange-Traded Funds (`mutual-funds-etfs`)
- **Scope**: SEBI 2017 categorisation circular, Large/Mid/Small cap definitions, Total Expense Ratio (TER) drag analysis, Direct vs Regular plans, index fund tracking error, ETF authorized participant liquidity mechanics, SIP/STP/SWP mathematics.
- **Key Questions**: What is the quantitative 25-year compounding penalty of Regular plan commissions?
- **Regulators**: SEBI, AMFI.
- **Planned Articles**: `sebi-mutual-fund-categorization-framework-explained`, `direct-vs-regular-mutual-funds-mathematical-drag-analysis`.

### 08. Stocks, Equity Research, and Quantitative Investing (`stocks-equity-research`)
- **Scope**: Fundamental company analysis, annual report dissection, notes to accounts, cash flow reconciliation, ROCE/ROE drivers, DuPont analysis, factor investing (Momentum, Low Volatility, Quality, Value) in Nifty indices.
- **Key Questions**: How to audit cash flow from operations versus reported net profit in Indian listed firms?
- **Regulators**: SEBI, BSE, NSE.
- **Planned Articles**: `how-to-read-an-indian-annual-report-and-notes-to-accounts`, `factor-investing-in-india-momentum-quality-low-volatility`.

### 09. Debt Investments and Fixed Income (`debt-fixed-income`)
- **Scope**: RBI Retail Direct platform, Sovereign G-Secs, Treasury Bills, State Development Loans (SDLs), corporate credit ratings, Macaulay duration, modified duration, yield curve shifts.
- **Key Questions**: How does an individual buy G-Secs directly via RBI Retail Direct without intermediary brokerage fees?
- **Regulators**: RBI, SEBI.
- **Planned Articles**: `rbi-retail-direct-complete-investor-guide-gsecs-tbills`, `bond-duration-yield-curve-and-interest-rate-risk-explained`.

### 10. Gold, Silver, and Commodities (`gold-commodities`)
- **Scope**: Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGBs) primary and secondary market mechanics, Section 47 capital gains exemptions, Gold ETFs, physical hallmarked bullion (BIS 916), digital gold counterparty risks.
- **Key Questions**: What is the exact secondary market discount and tax treatment of SGBs bought from stock exchanges?
- **Regulators**: RBI, SEBI, Ministry of Finance.
- **Planned Articles**: `sovereign-gold-bonds-sgb-complete-guide-secondary-market`, `gold-etfs-vs-physical-gold-vs-digital-gold-in-india`.

### 11. Real Estate, Land, and Property Finance (`real-estate-property`)
- **Scope**: Residential economics, rental yields vs cost of capital, home loan amortization, Section 24b and Section 80EEA, RERA compliance and homebuyer protections, SEBI REITs and Small & Medium (SM) REITs.
- **Key Questions**: Is buying real estate mathematically superior to renting and investing the surplus in equity in top Indian metros?
- **Regulators**: SEBI, RERA authorities.
- **Planned Articles**: `buying-vs-renting-in-india-mathematical-decision-framework`, `indian-reits-and-sm-reits-complete-investor-guide`.

### 12. Alternative and Private Investments (`alternative-investments`)
- **Scope**: Alternative Investment Funds (AIF Category I, II, III), Portfolio Management Services (PMS), unlisted pre-IPO shares, invoice discounting, P2P lending under RBI guidelines.
- **Key Questions**: What are the minimum capital commitments, taxation pass-through rules, and liquidity locks of Category II AIFs?
- **Regulators**: SEBI, RBI.
- **Planned Articles**: `portfolio-management-services-pms-vs-mutual-funds`, `alternative-investment-funds-aif-cat-1-2-3-explained`.

### 13. Insurance and Risk Management (`insurance-risk-management`)
- **Scope**: Pure term life insurance underwriting, Human Life Value (HLV) computation, Section 45 Insurance Act 1938 non-contestability, comprehensive health insurance (room rent caps, copays, restoration benefits, waiting periods), super top-up structures.
- **Key Questions**: How does Section 45 prevent an insurer from repudiating a claim after 3 consecutive years?
- **Regulators**: Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI).
- **Planned Articles**: `pure-term-insurance-blueprint-for-indian-families`, `health-insurance-selection-guide-room-rent-copay-riders`.

### 14. Retirement and Financial Independence (`retirement-fire`)
- **Scope**: National Pension System (NPS Tier 1 & 2), mandatory annuity requirements, EPF/VPF compounding, PPF 15-year rules, Safe Withdrawal Rate (SWR) modeling for India, sequence-of-returns risk.
- **Key Questions**: Can a 3.0%-3.5% SWR sustain a 35-year retirement in India amidst 7% inflation?
- **Regulators**: PFRDA, EPFO, CBDT.
- **Planned Articles**: `fire-in-india-safe-withdrawal-rates-and-corpus-sizing`, `national-pension-system-nps-tier-1-and-2-complete-guide`.

### 15. Business Ownership and Entrepreneurship (`business-entrepreneurship`)
- **Scope**: Legal business structures in India (Sole Proprietorship, LLP, Private Limited), GST registration triggers, reverse founder vesting, ESOP pool formation, Section 44ADA presumptive taxation.
- **Key Questions**: When should an Indian independent consultant or software engineer form an LLP or Pvt Ltd?
- **Regulators**: Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA), CBDT.
- **Planned Articles**: `sole-proprietorship-vs-llp-vs-pvt-ltd-for-indian-founders`, `section-44ada-presumptive-taxation-for-freelancers-and-consultants`.

### 16. Advanced Portfolio Construction (`portfolio-construction`)
- **Scope**: Strategic and tactical asset allocation, rebalancing bands (calendar vs 5/25 threshold), multi-asset correlation matrices, maximum drawdown mitigation, life-cycle glide paths.
- **Key Questions**: How does threshold rebalancing enhance risk-adjusted CAGR across Indian equity and debt market cycles?
- **Regulators**: SEBI.
- **Planned Articles**: `asset-allocation-frameworks-for-indian-investors`, `rebalancing-strategies-calendar-vs-threshold-band-methods`.

### 17. International Finance and Cross-Border Investing (`international-investing`)
- **Scope**: RBI Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS), Tax Collected at Source (TCS 20%), foreign equity taxation, mandatory ITR Schedule FA (Foreign Assets) reporting and Black Money Act implications, US estate tax for Indian residents.
- **Key Questions**: What are the criminal and financial penalties for failing to report foreign tech employer RSUs in Schedule FA?
- **Regulators**: RBI, CBDT.
- **Planned Articles**: `investing-in-us-stocks-from-india-lrs-tcs-and-taxation`, `itr-schedule-fa-foreign-assets-reporting-compliance-guide`.

### 18. Digital Assets and Emerging Financial Products (`digital-assets`)
- **Scope**: Virtual Digital Assets (VDAs) under Section 115BBH (30% flat tax without loss set-off), Section 194S 1% TDS, Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU-IND) exchange compliance, RBI Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC / e-Rupee).
- **Key Questions**: What are the statutory compliance rules for crypto asset reporting and TDS reconciliation in India?
- **Regulators**: CBDT, FIU-IND, RBI.
- **Planned Articles**: `crypto-taxation-in-india-section-115bbh-and-194s-guide`, `rbi-digital-rupee-cbdc-architecture-and-implications`.

### 19. Financial Fraud, Investor Rights, and Dispute Resolution (`fraud-investor-rights`)
- **Scope**: Multi-tier grievance mechanisms, RBI Integrated Ombudsman (CMS), SEBI SCORES 2.0 and SMART ODR, National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (Helpline 1930), Consumer Protection Act 2019.
- **Key Questions**: What is the step-by-step protocol to recover stolen funds within the cyber fraud 'golden hour' via 1930?
- **Regulators**: RBI, SEBI, Ministry of Home Affairs (I4C).
- **Planned Articles**: `rbi-integrated-ombudsman-scheme-escalation-blueprint`, `sebi-scores-2-and-smart-odr-investor-dispute-resolution`.

### 20. Family Wealth, Inheritance, and Estate Planning (`family-wealth-estate`)
- **Scope**: Will drafting and execution, witness requirements, probate jurisdictions (Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai), Nominee vs Legal Heir statutory superiority, Private Family Trusts, Hindu Succession Act coparcenary rules.
- **Key Questions**: Why does a Supreme Court precedent establish that a bank nominee cannot override a testamentary legal heir?
- **Regulators**: State Registration Authorities, Civil Courts.
- **Planned Articles**: `nominee-vs-legal-heir-in-india-legal-ownership-realities`, `drafting-a-legally-valid-will-and-probate-process-in-india`.

### 21. Financial Data, Calculators, and Personal-Finance Technology (`fintech-calculators`)
- **Scope**: Closed-form mathematical formulas for loan amortization, SIP future value, SWP capital exhaustion, Old vs New tax regime algorithms, open financial data.
- **Key Questions**: What is the exact mathematical amortization schedule formula used by Indian commercial lenders?
- **Regulators**: RBI, CBDT.
- **Planned Articles**: `mathematical-formulas-behind-every-personal-finance-calculator`, `loan-prepayment-amortization-mathematics-and-algorithms`.

### 22. Practical Financial Playbooks and Goal-Based Guides (`playbooks-guides`)
- **Scope**: End-to-end financial blueprints for major life milestones: starting a career, buying a first home, marriage, planning for higher education, managing unexpected inheritance or liquidity windfalls.
- **Key Questions**: What is the prioritized 10-step financial sequence for a college graduate entering the Indian corporate workforce?
- **Regulators**: Cross-regulatory coordination.
- **Planned Articles**: `first-job-personal-finance-playbook-for-indian-graduates`, `handling-a-financial-windfall-esop-inheritance-protocol`.
