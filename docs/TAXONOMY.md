## 2. Audio-Native Prose Mandate

In accordance with project editorial policy for text-to-speech conversion and audiobook listening:
1. **Continuous Spoken Prose**: All articles must be written in flowing, natural English paragraphs rather than isolated mathematical formulas (like LaTeX blocks) or complex visual table traps.
2. **Spoken Mathematical Relationships**: Formulas must be transcribed into spoken English. For example, compounding equations are expressed as: "The future value of an investment is calculated by multiplying the starting principal by the factor of one plus the periodic rate of return raised to the power of the total number of compounding periods."
3. **Substantive Scale**: Every primary guide is planned as an exhaustive treatise exceeding **10,000 substantive words**, eliminating superficial summaries in favor of deep statutory analysis.

## 3. Comprehensive Domain Specifications

### Domain A — Personal Finance Foundations (`pf-foundations`)
- **Scope**: Compounding mathematics, time value of money, real versus nominal returns, purchasing power dynamics (official CPI versus aspirational urban inflation in Indian metros), balance sheet construction, cashflow budgeting architectures, emergency liquidity reserves, and psychological cognitive biases in financial decisions.
- **Exclusions**: Specific stock valuation models, detailed tax filing procedures, corporate balance sheet auditing.
- **Statutory & Primary Sources**: Reserve Bank of India, Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI).
- **Key Relationships**: Links directly to `banking-deposits` (Domain B) for liquid emergency reserves and `career-income-capital` (Domain T) for earning capacity.

### Domain B — Banking and Deposit Products (`banking-deposits`)
- **Scope**: Scheduled commercial banks (public, private, foreign), small finance banks, payments banks, urban and rural co-operative banks, savings accounts, current accounts, fixed deposits, recurring deposits, sweep-in accounts, deposit insurance under the DICGC Act 1961, Section 18A interim payouts (90-day mandate), daily-product savings interest calculations, TDS under Section 194A, and dormant account claims through RBI UDGAM.
- **Exclusions**: Interbank settlement switch protocols, corporate syndicate loans.
- **Statutory & Primary Sources**: Reserve Bank of India (RBI), Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation (DICGC).
- **Flagship Treatise**: `dicgc-deposit-insurance-architecture-limits-and-ownership-structures` (11,021 words).

### Domain C — Payments and Digital Financial Infrastructure (`payments-digital-infrastructure`)
- **Scope**: NPCI retail payment systems, Unified Payments Interface (UPI 2.0), interchange frameworks, merchant discount rates (MDR), NEFT and RTGS 24x7 clearing and settlement cycles, RuPay card schemes, NACH electronic mandates, BBPS bill payment integration, FASTag toll networks, RBI payment aggregator (PA/PG) escrow regulations, chargeback rights, and digital payment fraud prevention.
- **Exclusions**: Blockchain cryptocurrency protocols, core banking ledger internal software engineering.
- **Statutory & Primary Sources**: Reserve Bank of India, National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI).

### Domain D — Credit and Borrowing (`credit-borrowing`)
- **Scope**: Credit information companies (CIBIL, Experian, CRIF High Mark, Equifax), scoring algorithms and credit mix weighting, home loan benchmarking (External Benchmark Lending Rate / EBLR vs MCLR), Loan Against Property (LAP), gold loans, education loans, vehicle financing, credit card billing cycles, APR compound interest math, revolving debt traps, RBI Digital Lending Guidelines (DLG) for loan service providers, and recovery agent codes of conduct.
- **Exclusions**: Corporate commercial paper, international project financing syndicates.
- **Statutory & Primary Sources**: Reserve Bank of India, TransUnion CIBIL, National Housing Bank (NHB).

### Domain E — Indian Government Savings and Small-Savings Schemes (`gov-small-savings`)
- **Scope**: Sovereign small savings schemes administered via Department of Posts and designated banks: Public Provident Fund (PPF), Sukanya Samriddhi Account (SSA), Senior Citizens Savings Scheme (SCSS), National Savings Certificate (NSC), Kisan Vikas Patra (KVP), Post Office Monthly Income Scheme (POMIS), Post Office Time Deposits (POTD), quarterly interest revision formulas based on secondary G-Sec yields (Shyamala Gopinath formula), tax status (EEE vs EET), and PPF statutory immunity from court attachment.
- **Exclusions**: Commercial bank fixed deposits, private corporate fixed deposits.
- **Statutory & Primary Sources**: Department of Economic Affairs (Ministry of Finance), Department of Posts (India Post), Reserve Bank of India.

### Domain F — Mutual Funds and Pooled Investments (`mutual-funds-pooled`)
- **Scope**: SEBI Categorization and Rationalization circulars (Equity, Debt, Hybrid, Solution-Oriented, Index Funds, ETFs, FoFs), Total Expense Ratio (TER) caps and wealth erosion mechanics, Direct versus Regular plans, systematic transactions (SIP, STP, SWP), tracking error and tracking difference, portfolio overlap, AMFI Code of Conduct, and capital gains taxation.
- **Exclusions**: Category III Alternative Investment Funds, Portfolio Management Services (PMS), direct individual stock trading.
- **Statutory & Primary Sources**: Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI), Association of Mutual Funds in India (AMFI).

### Domain G — Equity Markets and Listed Securities (`equity-markets-listed-securities`)
- **Scope**: National Stock Exchange (NSE), Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE), primary markets (IPOs, FPOs, Offer for Sale, Rights Issues), secondary markets, rolling settlement cycles (T+1, optional T+0), demat accounts, depository participants (NSDL, CDSL), clearing corporations (NCL, ICCL), securities lending and borrowing (SLB), corporate actions (splits, bonuses, buybacks, dividends), minority shareholder rights, and insider trading prohibitions under SEBI PIT Regulations.
- **Exclusions**: Commodity futures contracts, unlisted private company shares.
- **Statutory & Primary Sources**: Securities and Exchange Board of India, National Stock Exchange, BSE Limited, Ministry of Corporate Affairs.

### Domain H — Fixed Income, Debt Markets and Credit Analysis (`fixed-income-debt-markets`)
- **Scope**: Sovereign debt (Treasury Bills, dated Government of India securities, State Development Loans), RBI Retail Direct platform, corporate debt (Non-Convertible Debentures, Commercial Paper, Certificates of Deposit, Tier-1 and Tier-2 bank bonds), credit rating agencies (CRISIL, ICRA, CARE, India Ratings), credit default risk, yield curve dynamics, Macaulay duration, modified duration, convexity, debt mutual fund indexation removal post-Finance Act 2023, and RFQ platforms.
- **Exclusions**: Small savings postal schemes, retail bank term deposits.
- **Statutory & Primary Sources**: Reserve Bank of India, SEBI, Fixed Income Money Market and Derivatives Association (FIMMDA), Clearing Corporation of India (CCIL).

### Domain I — Insurance and Risk Protection (`insurance-risk-protection`)
- **Scope**: Pure term life insurance, Married Women's Property Act endorsements under Section 6, Section 45 incontestability clause of Insurance Act 1938, general and health insurance (indemnity, room rent sub-limits, proportionate deduction, pre-existing disease waiting periods, moratorium period rules, super top-up deductibles, cashless claim authorization), motor third-party and own damage coverage, and IRDAI grievance redressal via Bima Bharosa and Insurance Ombudsman.
- **Exclusions**: Pure wealth-accumulation claims of ULIPs (analyzed strictly as high-cost bundled products), foreign insurance policies.
- **Statutory & Primary Sources**: Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI), Council for Insurance Ombudsmen.

### Domain J — Retirement, Pensions and Social Protection (`retirement-pensions`)
- **Scope**: Statutory social security: Employees' Provident Fund (EPF, VPF, EPS-95 under EPFO), wage ceilings, Section 10(11)/10(12) interest taxation beyond ₹2.5 Lakh/₹5 Lakh annual contributions, National Pension System (NPS Tier I and Tier II under PFRDA), Pension Fund Managers (PFMs), asset allocation choices (Active versus Auto Choice), 60% tax-free lump sum withdrawal, 40% mandatory annuity purchase, annuity product types, Atal Pension Yojana (APY), and post-retirement withdrawal strategies (Safe Withdrawal Rates in India).
- **Exclusions**: Foreign social security systems, corporate golden handshakes.
- **Statutory & Primary Sources**: Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO), Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA), Income Tax Department.

### Domain K — Indian Taxation (`indian-taxation`)
- **Scope**: Direct taxation: Income-tax Act 1961, Section 115BAC (New Tax Regime vs Old Tax Regime), salary income (standard deduction, HRA exemption under Section 10(13A)), capital gains restructuring under Finance (No. 2) Act 2024 (STCG 20%, LTCG 12.5%, Section 112A ₹1.25 Lakh exemption, removal of indexation), interest income taxation, dividend taxation under Section 56(2)(i), set-off and carry-forward of losses, Advance Tax schedules and interest under Sections 234A/234B/234C, TDS under Sections 194A/194C/194J/194IA, TCS on LRS under Section 206C(1G), and GST principles for freelancers and small businesses.
- **Exclusions**: Customs tariff litigations, multinational transfer pricing disputes.
- **Statutory & Primary Sources**: Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT), Income Tax Department, Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC).

### Domain L — Real Estate, Property and Land (`real-estate-property-land`)
- **Scope**: Real estate transactions in India: Title verification, 30-year search, encumbrance certificates (EC), Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act 2016 (RERA), state RERA authorities, circle rates (guidance values / ready reckoner rates) versus market agreement values, stamp duty and registration fees, capital gains under Section 50C and Section 54/54EC/54F, rental yields, Section 24(b) home loan interest deduction, Section 80C principal deduction, Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs), Small and Medium REITs (SM REITs under SEBI), fractional ownership, and joint development agreements (JDA).
- **Exclusions**: Architectural structural engineering, interior decorating services.
- **Statutory & Primary Sources**: Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (RERA), State Revenue Departments, SEBI, Income Tax Department.

### Domain M — Gold, Silver and Commodities (`gold-silver-commodities`)
- **Scope**: Precious metals and commodity investing: Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) hallmarking (6-digit HUID), physical gold coins/bars, making charges and spread erosion, Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGB issue terms, 2.50% semi-annual interest, Section 47 capital gains exemption on maturity, secondary market trading on NSE/BSE), Gold ETFs, Gold FoFs, Silver ETFs, multi-asset allocation funds, Multi Commodity Exchange (MCX), commodity futures contracts, import duties, and capital gains taxation under Finance Act 2024.
- **Exclusions**: Commercial mining operations, international physical bullion smuggling.
- **Statutory & Primary Sources**: Reserve Bank of India, Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS), SEBI, Multi Commodity Exchange (MCX).

### Domain N — Alternative and Non-Traditional Investments (`alternative-investments`)
- **Scope**: SEBI Alternative Investment Funds Regulations 2012: Category I AIFs (Venture Capital, Angel Funds, Social Impact), Category II AIFs (Private Equity, Private Debt, Real Estate Funds), Category III AIFs (Hedge Funds, Complex Trading Strategies), minimum ₹1 Crore investment threshold (₹25 Lakh for accredited investors/angel funds), unlisted shares and pre-IPO secondary markets, employee stock options (ESOPs) and Section 17(2) perquisite taxation, asset leasing, equipment financing, and art/collectibles.
- **Exclusions**: Unregulated collective investment schemes, illegal multi-level marketing.
- **Statutory & Primary Sources**: Securities and Exchange Board of India, Ministry of Corporate Affairs, Income Tax Department.

### Domain O — P2P Lending and Private Credit (`p2p-lending-private-credit`)
- **Scope**: RBI Non-Banking Financial Company - Peer to Peer Lending Platform (Reserve Bank) Directions 2017 and August 2024 amendments: ₹50 Lakh aggregate lending cap across platforms, net worth certificates, ₹50,000 single-borrower cap, prohibition on credit guarantees, ban on assured returns and instant-liquidity sweep products, escrow account mechanics (lender and borrower escrows), Trade Receivables Discounting System (TReDS under RBI), invoice discounting platforms, expected credit loss (ECL), and default recovery reality.
- **Exclusions**: Informal moneylending, fraudulent deposit-taking.
- **Statutory & Primary Sources**: Reserve Bank of India, Receivables Exchange of India (RXIL).

### Domain P — Derivatives, Leverage and Advanced Trading (`derivatives-trading`)
- **Scope**: Exchange-traded derivatives on NSE and BSE: Index futures and options (NIFTY, BANKNIFTY), stock derivatives, option pricing models (Black-Scholes-Merton), option Greeks (Delta, Gamma, Theta, Vega, Rho), implied volatility (IV) and India VIX, margin frameworks (SPAN, Extreme Loss Margin, Peak Margin), SEBI retail F&O loss studies (93% retail traders experiencing net losses), weekly option expiry contract rationalization, hedging techniques, covered calls, protective puts, and Section 43(5) non-speculative business income classification.
- **Exclusions**: Illegal dabba trading, unregulated binary betting apps.
- **Statutory & Primary Sources**: Securities and Exchange Board of India, National Stock Exchange, Income Tax Department.

### Domain Q — Portfolio Management and Quantitative Finance (`portfolio-management-quant`)
- **Scope**: Quantitative portfolio construction: Strategic and tactical asset allocation, Modern Portfolio Theory (MPT), efficient frontier, Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM), factor investing (Value, Momentum, Quality, Low Volatility on NSE indices), risk-adjusted performance metrics (Sharpe ratio, Sortino ratio, Treynor ratio, Information ratio, Maximum Drawdown, Value at Risk / VaR, Expected Shortfall / CVaR), rebalancing bands and tax friction, and portfolio optimization techniques.
- **Exclusions**: Astrological trading forecasts, unverified black-box signal bots.
- **Statutory & Primary Sources**: SEBI, NSE Indices, Reserve Bank of India.

### Domain R — Financial Fraud, Scams, Forensic Finance and Shadow Economy (`financial-fraud-scams-shadow-economy`)

## 4. Rules for Adding Topics and Resolving Duplicates

1. **Evidence-First Requirement**: A new topic or category cannot be introduced purely from speculative recall. It must be grounded in an authoritative primary statutory Act, regulatory circular, official gazette, or demonstrated market reality.
2. **Canonical Disambiguation**: When multiple terms refer to the identical economic or legal concept (such as "Guidance Value", "Ready Reckoner Rate", and "Circle Rate"), one canonical concept ID is designated (`circle-rate-guidance-value`) and all alternative regional and colloquial variants are registered as immutable aliases.
3. **Pillar Cross-Linking over Redundancy**: When a foundational concept (like Macaulay duration) is relevant across multiple domains (such as Bond Markets, Debt Mutual Funds, and Asset Allocation), the technical definition resides canonically within Domain H (`fixed-income-debt-markets`), while other domains cross-reference it with contextual summaries rather than duplicating thousands of identical words.
