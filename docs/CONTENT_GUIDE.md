# Dhanpedia India — Content Guide & Audio-First Editorial Standards

## 1. Editorial Voice & Governing Philosophy

Dhanpedia India is engineered as an exhaustive, authoritative, deeply researched reference encyclopedia. The platform is designed from the ground up to be **audiobook-first and text-to-speech (TTS) native**, ensuring that every article sounds natural, engaging, and effortless when read aloud by synthetic voices, Microsoft Edge Read Aloud, and speech-to-text converters.

### Core Authorial Mandates:
1. **Uncompromised Exhaustive Depth (>10,000 Words)**: Every substantive foundational article must be an encyclopedic masterwork of at least **10,000 substantive words**. Rather than shallow summaries or brief listicles, articles provide full historical context, complete statutory mechanics, judicial jurisprudence, behavioral psychology, and practical implementation frameworks.
2. **Natural Spoken Prose (Paragraph-First Format)**: Avoid isolated mathematical formulas, complex LaTeX equations, raw ASCII grids, and fragmented bullet points that break the flow of an audiobook. All calculations, ratios, variables, and statutory rules must be explained in continuous, rhythmic, beautiful English prose.
3. **Primary Evidence & Rigorous Research**: Prior to writing any analysis, exhaustive research across primary statutory records (The Gazette of India, RBI Master Directions, SEBI Circulars, CBDT Notifications, Supreme Court judgments) must be conducted and documented.

## 2. Audio-Friendly Writing Rules for Complex Finance

When explaining mathematical models, legal statutes, and regulatory mechanisms for an audio listener:

### 2.1 Spoken Formulas Instead of Isolated Mathematical Notation
- **Avoid**: $FV = PV \times (1 + r)^n$
- **Mandatory Spoken Format**: 
  > *"To determine the future value of an investment, multiply the initial principal amount by the sum of one plus the periodic rate of return, raised to the power of the number of compounding intervals. For example, if you place one hundred thousand rupees at an annual compound interest rate of eight percent for twenty years, the factor of one point zero eight multiplied by itself twenty times produces four point six six. Multiplying this factor by your initial principal yields a terminal sum of four hundred and sixty-six thousand and ninety-five rupees."*

### 2.2 Narrative Numerical Walkthroughs Instead of Visual Tables
- **Avoid**: Sticking critical information exclusively inside raw visual table grids that audio readers stumble over.
- **Mandatory Spoken Format**: 
  > *"Let us examine how deposit insurance is calculated across distinct ownership capacities for an investor named Rohan holding accounts at a single commercial bank. In his sole personal savings and fixed deposit accounts, Rohan maintains an aggregate balance of five hundred and fifty-two thousand and seven hundred rupees. Because the statutory ceiling under the Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation Act is capped at five lakh rupees per depositor in the same legal right and capacity, the Corporation disburses exactly five hundred thousand rupees, leaving the remaining fifty-two thousand and seven hundred rupees as an unsecured residual claim against the liquidator. However, in a second account held jointly where Rohan is the primary titular holder and his spouse Priya is the second holder, the law treats this joint ownership as an entirely distinct legal person..."*

### 2.3 Pronounceable Headings & Smooth Transitions
- Use self-contained, descriptive, pronounceable headings (e.g., *"How Section Eighteen A Guarantees Compensation Within Ninety Days"* rather than *"Sec. 18A"*).
- Always spell out numbers and currency in spoken prose when introducing critical statutory thresholds (e.g., *"five lakh rupees"* alongside *"₹5,00,000"*).
- Every acronym must be smoothly expanded upon first mention (e.g., *"the Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation, commonly referred to as the DICGC"*).

## 3. The 14-Part Layered Article Architecture (Audio-Optimized)

Every substantive >10,000-word article follows this rigorous structure:

1. **Executive Orientation and Spoken Summary**: A sweeping, high-density audio overview explaining the significance of the topic.
2. **Historical Evolution & Statutory Genesis**: The legislative origin, parliamentary debates, historical bank failures, and economic crises that necessitated the law.
3. **Statutory Roots & Foundational Legal Definitions**: Exact analysis of statutory sections, regulatory mandates, and gazette notifications.
4. **Underlying Operational Mechanics**: How the infrastructure operates internally, including regulatory reporting, premium assessments, and liquidity buffers.
5. **Practical Indian Implementation**: Step-by-step procedural reality for Indian citizens, salaried workers, NRI depositors, and businesses.
6. **Narrative Worked Calculations & Case Walkthroughs**: Deep numerical examples explained fully in conversational, spoken mathematical prose.
7. **Legal Capacities & Strategic Structuring**: How ownership capacities (sole, joint permutations, minors, trusts, partnerships) function under Indian law.
8. **Statutory Timeframes & Resolution Protocols**: Exact legal timelines (such as the 45-day submission and 90-day settlement mandate under Section 18A).
9. **Comparative Analysis Across Alternative Financial Vehicles**: In-depth narrative comparison against competing instruments across risk, liquidity, and return.
10. **Taxation & Compliance Framework**: Treatment under the Income-tax Act, TDS provisions, advance tax schedules, and ITR reporting requirements.
11. **Historical Crises & Real-World Case Studies**: Detailed historical analysis of real bank resolutions (e.g., Palai Central Bank, Global Trust Bank, PMC Bank, Yes Bank, Lakshmi Vilas Bank).
12. **Common Traps, Systemic Vulnerabilities & Misconceptions**: Structural failure scenarios, mis-selling myths, and consumer illusions.
13. **Frequently Asked Questions**: Conversational questions and exhaustive spoken answers addressing real-world investor dilemmas.
14. **Primary Statutory Citations & Verification Record**: Complete references to statutory acts, gazette notifications, and master circulars.
