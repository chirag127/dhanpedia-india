# Dhanpedia India — Agent Content Editorial Policy & Quality Standards

**Status:** Canonical Specialist Policy

---

## 1. Prime Editorial Directive

Dhanpedia India is engineered as an authoritative reference encyclopedia for personal wealth accumulation, corporate structuring, and the Indian financial system. It serves advanced investors, salaried professionals, software engineers, and founders who demand exhaustive, legally rigorous analysis.

The encyclopedia strictly rejects superficial, SEO-driven content farming. Every published article must serve as a comprehensive, durable reference publication.

---

## 2. The 10,000-Word Substantive Depth Standard

1. **Foundational Treatise Benchmark:** All major treatises published under `src/content/articles/` must exceed **10,000 words** of original, non-duplicative, deeply researched prose.
2. **Quality Over Quantity:** Word count is an indicator of exhaustive structural depth, never of artificial padding. If an article reaches 10,000 words, it must do so because it thoroughly investigates:
   - Theoretical foundations and historical economic context.
   - Statutory and legal architecture (exact Acts, sections, rules, and circulars).
   - Operational mechanics and banking/market infrastructure.
   - Tax implications under both Old and New Tax Regimes (Finance Act 2024).
   - Forensic failure modes, systemic risks, and shadow economy dynamics.
   - Step-by-step mathematical calculations with spoken narrative formulas.
   - Comparative institutional analysis across public, private, and foreign entities.
   - Dispute resolution, ombudsman procedures, and litigation precedents.

---

## 3. Structural Blueprint for Master Treatises

Every substantial treatise must follow a layered pedagogical architecture:

```
[Layer 1: Executive Overview & Core Proposition]
  ├── Concise plain-English definition
  └── Strategic importance to Indian wealth accumulation

[Layer 2: Statutory and Institutional Framework]
  ├── Governing Parliamentary Acts and primary regulators
  └── Recent circulars, notifications, and gazette amendments

[Layer 3: Operational Mechanics & Infrastructure]
  ├── Core entities, settlement cycles, and clearing systems
  └── Ownership structures and capital safeguards

[Layer 4: Quantitative Models & Spoken Mathematics]
  ├── Spoken formula narration (audio-native)
  └── Concrete numerical scenarios (Rs. in Lakhs and Crores)

[Layer 5: Taxation and Compliance (Finance Act 2024)]
  ├── Tax treatment across holding periods and income slabs
  └── Section 87A, 112, 112A, 115BAC, and TDS/TCS mechanisms

[Layer 6: Forensic Risks, Failure Modes & Shadow Economy]
  ├── Institutional distress, liquidity freezes, and fraud vectors
  └── Real-world shadow practices (cash on-money, dabba trading, unapproved apps)

[Layer 7: Redressal, Ombudsman & Practical Checklist]
  ├── Step-by-step grievance escalation (Internal Ombudsman -> RBI/SEBI -> Judicial)
  └── Pragmatic reader decision matrix
```

---

## 4. Academic Rigor and Factual Integrity

- **Prohibition on Speculation:** Speculative price projections, guaranteed return claims, or get-rich-quick heuristics are strictly forbidden.
- **Traceability:** Every statutory claim must link to an active entry in [`data/claims/claim-registry.json`](file:///c:/g/dhanpedia-india/data/claims/claim-registry.json) and cite an entry in [`data/sources/source-registry.json`](file:///c:/g/dhanpedia-india/data/sources/source-registry.json).
- **Educational Disclaimer:** Clear disclaimers stating that the material constitutes financial education and statutory reference, not individualized investment, legal, or tax advice.
